# Hook

Kingstack is a game that can be played with 2-4 players and a standard 52 plus 4 joker cards. Players organize their army into 4 stacks and must make tactical decisions to protect their joker card, the flag, and take out their opponents' flags.

Each stack is played face down with the top card being revealed only once combat is initiated! Plan, coordinate, bluff, and trick your way to the top of the stack, the Kingstack!

---
# Motivation

Kingstack is a game of hidden information and tactical maneuvers. The inspiration came from the question "how can you add re-playability to a game without randomization?" 

I started to do some research into "hidden information" games and realized there was actually very few popular games utilizing the mechanic. The most prominent game would be [Stratego]([Stratego - Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stratego), which a good chunk of my inspiration came from.

Another restriction I placed on myself in designing Kingstack was that I wanted the game to be playable with a single deck of cards. There are not many strategy games out there played with a deck of cards which just made the challenge more tantalizing to me.
